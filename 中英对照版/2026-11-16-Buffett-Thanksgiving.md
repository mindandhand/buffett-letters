BERKSHIRE HATHAWAY INC.

NEWS RELEASE

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE November 16, 2026

Omaha, NE (BRK.A; BRK.B) – Today, Warren E. Buffett, Chairman Emeritus, will convert 6,150 Class A shares into 9,225,000 Class B shares and will donate these shares to four family foundations: 3,690,000 shares to The Susan Thompson Buffett Foundation and 1,845,000 shares to each of The Sherwood Foundation, The Howard G. Buffett Foundation, and NoVo Foundation.

Following the donations, Mr. Buffett’s ownership of Berkshire will consist of 182,140 Class A shares and 1,162 Class B shares.

---

Mr. Buffett’s comments follow:

“Today’s gift is consistent with the plan I have previously described. All my Berkshire shares will be donated to the four foundations, with the goal of growing the grants each year. Reaching this goal will largely depend on stock market conditions between now and the target date. My children have given thoughtfully to worthy causes for decades, and I trust 100% both their abilities and their fidelity.

You heard plenty from me in September about my confidence in Berkshire. That confidence is as strong as ever, so I will keep this Thanksgiving message short, and emphasize just one thought from last year.

Last Thanksgiving I wrote that kindness is costless but also priceless. A year later, I would only add that it compounds, and at a better rate than anything Charlie and I ever bought. Both of us received beyond measurement costless benefits from a few teachers such as Ben Graham, Joe Rosenfield and Tom Murphy. Because of this we not only prospered mightily but were able to pass along many of the benefits we received.

Much of my good fortune has come in the form of people who were kind to me and expected nothing in return. I’m grateful to every one of them.

This Thanksgiving, thank one of those selfless people in your own life. Better yet, be a benefactor for someone else.”

## 新闻稿

立即发布 2026年11月16日

内布拉斯加州奥马哈（BRK.A；BRK.B）——今天，名誉董事长沃伦·E·巴菲特将把6,150股A类股转换为9,225,000股B类股，并将这些股份捐赠给四家家族基金会：向苏珊·汤普森·巴菲特基金会捐赠3,690,000股，向舍伍德基金会、霍华德·G·巴菲特基金会和NoVo基金会各捐赠1,845,000股。

捐赠完成后，巴菲特先生将持有182,140股伯克希尔A类股和1,162股B类股。

以下是巴菲特先生的说明：

“今天的捐赠，与我先前说明的计划一致。我持有的全部伯克希尔股票，都将捐赠给这四家基金会，目标是每年增加捐赠。能否达到这个目标，很大程度上取决于从现在到目标日期之间的股市状况。几十年来，我的孩子们一直用心支持有价值的事业，我对他们的能力与忠诚，都有100%的信任。

今年9月，我已经向你们说了不少自己对伯克希尔的信心。这份信心仍然丝毫未减。因此，这次感恩节寄语就简短一点，只强调去年提过的一个想法。

去年感恩节，我写道，善意不花钱，却价值无穷。一年过去，我只想加上一点：善意也会复利增长，而且回报率比查理和我买过的任何东西都更高。本·格雷厄姆、乔·罗森菲尔德和汤姆·墨菲等几位老师，都曾无偿给予我们两人难以估量的帮助。因此，我们不仅获得了丰厚成果，也得以把收到的许多好处传递下去。

我的许多好运，都来自那些善待我、却不求回报的人。我感激他们每一位。

这个感恩节，请向自己生命中这样一位无私的人道谢。更好的办法，是成为另一个人的恩人。”
