### English

BERKSHIRE HATHAWAY INC.

NEWS RELEASE

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE May 5, 2025

Omaha, NE (BRK.A; BRK.B) –

At a question and answer session that preceded Berkshire Hathaway’s Annual Shareholders Meeting that was held on May 3, 2025, Warren Buffett Berkshire’s Chairman and CEO announced that he was going to recommend to Berkshire’s Board of Directors at its meeting to be held on the next day that Greg Abel, Berkshire’s Vice Chairman Non-Insurance Operations be appointed Berkshire’s Chief Executive Officer to become effective on January 1, 2026. On May 4, 2025, Berkshire’s Board of Directors voted unanimously to appoint Greg Abel to become Berkshire’s President and CEO effective on January 1, 2026. Warren Buffett will remain the Chairman of the Board of Directors.

## About Berkshire

Berkshire Hathaway and its subsidiaries engage in diverse business activities including insurance and reinsurance, utilities and energy, freight rail transportation, manufacturing, services and retailing. Common stock of the company is listed on the New York Stock Exchange, trading symbols BRK.A and BRK.B.

### 中文

伯克希尔·哈撒韦公司

## 新闻稿

立即发布 2025年5月5日

内布拉斯加州奥马哈（BRK.A；BRK.B）——

在2025年5月3日举行的伯克希尔年度股东大会之前的问答环节中，伯克希尔董事长兼首席执行官沃伦·巴菲特宣布，他将在次日召开的董事会上，建议任命公司负责非保险业务的副董事长格雷格·阿贝尔担任首席执行官，自2026年1月1日起生效。2025年5月4日，伯克希尔董事会一致表决通过，任命格雷格·阿贝尔担任公司总裁兼首席执行官，自2026年1月1日起生效。沃伦·巴菲特将继续担任董事长。

## 关于伯克希尔

伯克希尔·哈撒韦及其子公司从事多种业务，包括保险与再保险、公用事业与能源、铁路货运、制造、服务及零售。公司普通股在纽约证券交易所上市，交易代码为BRK.A和BRK.B。
